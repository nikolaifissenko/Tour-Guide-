### Descrizione completa (1047/3000)
Rome is best understood from above. This private golf cart tour links four of the city's finest viewpoints, from the Pincio in the north to the Janiculum Hill in the west, and passes Rome's most famous landmarks on the way between them.

Your driver picks you up at your hotel or any address in central Rome. Start on the Pincio Terrace, high above Piazza del Popolo, with a coffee and a maritozzo, Rome's traditional cream-filled bun. Then ride down into the historic center, with stops near the Trevi Fountain, the Pantheon, and Piazza Navona.

Continue past Piazza Venezia, the Roman Forum, the Colosseum, and the Circus Maximus, then climb the Aventine Hill. Walk through the Orange Garden for a view over the Tiber, and peek through the famous Aventine Keyhole, which frames the dome of St. Peter's.

Cross the river to the Janiculum Hill. Stop at the monumental Fontana dell'Acqua Paola and its terrace, then continue to the top of the hill for the widest panorama over Rome's domes and rooftops. Your driver drops you off wherever you like.

### In evidenza
- Enjoy 4 of Rome's best views, from the Pincio to the Janiculum Hill
