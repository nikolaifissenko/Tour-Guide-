## Perché non basta "highlights + consumazione"

Ricerca 2026-10-19: ogni combinazione highlights + cibo in golf cart a Roma esiste già.
Gelato (Wheel Tours, ItalyPerfect, GYG t560865 con supplì), espresso + pizza + supplì +
maritozzo/tiramisù (Guruwalk, byFood, Expedia), espresso + maritozzo privato
(Travelcurious), prosecco al Gianicolo (Headout, Musement VIP), pasta in trattoria (AAA/
Viator), cena + gelato di sera (GYG t563277). Il 1522784 aveva già una consumazione
(gelato/grattachecca) ed è stato rifiutato lo stesso.

Quindi la consumazione **non può essere la differenza**: deve servire la differenza.
Leva scelta: **pubblico poco servito** (anziani, mobilità ridotta, famiglie con
passeggino), già usata nella versione Easy-Access. La pausa caffè diventa la **sosta da
seduti a metà percorso** che questo pubblico ha bisogno di fare, non un assaggio.
Nessun listing esistente in golf cart è costruito su accessibilità + sosta seduta.

Rischio residuo: resta un highlights in una categoria satura. Se GYG rifiuta anche
questo, la via è il prodotto tematico (`gyg-coppede-villa-torlonia.md`) o un altro canale.

### Titolo (56/60)
Rome: Easy-Access Golf Cart Highlights with Coffee Break

### Descrizione breve (173/200)
See Rome's icons without the long walks on a private golf cart tour. Every stop is steps from the cart, with a seated Roman coffee break and pickup anywhere in central Rome.

### Descrizione completa (1031/3000)
Rome's highlights are spread across miles of cobblestones, hills, and stairs. This private golf cart tour brings them to you. It's designed for travelers who prefer not to walk far, including seniors, guests with limited mobility, and families with small children.

Your driver picks you up at your hotel or any address in central Rome. Ride past the Roman Forum and loop around the Colosseum and the Arch of Constantine, with a photo stop at the best viewpoint. Continue past the Circus Maximus and the Mouth of Truth, then head to the Pantheon.

Halfway through, take a seated break at a historic café near the Pantheon with an espresso or cappuccino and a maritozzo, Rome's traditional cream-filled bun. Then continue to Piazza Navona and the Trevi Fountain. At each stop, the cart parks just a short walk away, and you set the pace.

Cross the river to the Janiculum Hill for a panoramic view over the city's domes and rooftops. Finish with a photo stop at St. Peter's Square before your driver drops you off wherever you like.

### In evidenza
- Reach every stop with only a short walk from the golf cart
- Take a seated break at a café with an espresso and a maritozzo
- See the Colosseum, Pantheon, Trevi Fountain, and St. Peter's in one ride
- Get picked up and dropped off at any address in central Rome
- Take in the panorama over Rome's rooftops from the Janiculum Hill

### Incluso
- Private electric golf cart with English-speaking driver
- Pickup and drop-off at any address in central Rome
- Seated coffee break: 1 hot drink and 1 maritozzo per person

### Escluso
- Tour guide
- Entry tickets to monuments
- Extra food and drinks
- Gratuities
