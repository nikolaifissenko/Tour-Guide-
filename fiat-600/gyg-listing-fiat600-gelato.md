Ride through Rome in a restored 1960s Fiat 600 convertible, roof down. Pass the Colosseum, peek through the Aventine Keyhole, and stop for artisan gelato on a private tour with a driver.

### Descrizione completa
Travel back to the Rome of La Dolce Vita in a sunshine-yellow Fiat 600 convertible from the 1960s. With the roof folded down and just your group on board, this private ride takes you through some of the city's most photogenic corners at an easy, unhurried pace.

Your driver picks you up at your hotel or any address in central Rome. Start with a photo stop at the Colle Oppio viewpoint, where the Colosseum fills the frame behind the car. Continue past the Arch of Constantine and along the Circus Maximus, with the ruins of the Palatine Hill rising above you.

Climb the quiet Aventine Hill, one of Rome's most elegant neighborhoods. Stroll into the Orange Garden for a panoramic view over the domes and rooftops, then visit the Knights of Malta square to look through the famous keyhole that frames St. Peter's dome.

Before heading back, stop at a traditional artisan gelateria for a cone of real Italian gelato, included in the price. Your driver then drops you off wherever you like in central Rome.

Ideal for couples, honeymooners, and anyone who wants unforgettable photos with a classic Italian car.
