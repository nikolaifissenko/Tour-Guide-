# Fiat 600 gialla (BA 351486) — prompt Gemini per aggiungere persone alle foto

> ⚠️ **Prima di caricarle su GYG/Airbnb:** Airbnb Experiences richiede foto reali dell'esperienza e vieta immagini generate/alterate con AI che rappresentano in modo fuorviante l'esperienza. GetYourGuide ha regole simili sull'autenticità (verificare le linee guida foto attuali). Rischio concreto: listing rifiutato o sospeso, recensioni "le foto non corrispondono". Uso sicuro: **social, mockup, shot list per un vero shooting.** Per i listing: shooting reale con 2–3 amici/primi clienti seguendo esattamente queste scene.

## Come usarli
1. Apri Gemini (modello immagini), carica **una foto alla volta**.
2. Incolla il **blocco base** + il **prompt della scena**.
3. Se il risultato è sbagliato, usa i **prompt di correzione** in fondo — non rigenerare da zero.

## Blocco base (incollare sempre prima del prompt scena)
```
Edit this exact photo. Keep the car 100% identical: same yellow paint, same chrome, same plate "351486 BA", same black folded soft top, same tan seats, same wheels. Keep the background, light, shadows and camera angle unchanged. Only add people as described below.
Scale is critical: this is a very small 1960s Fiat 600 — adults sit low, their shoulders and heads rise above the door line, their knees are close to the dashboard. People must be correctly sized for this tiny car.
Style: natural, candid travel photography, golden-hour sunlight matching the existing shadows, real skin texture, no posing for the camera unless stated, no text, no logos, no watermark. Photorealistic, like an iPhone photo taken by a friend.
```

## Prompt per scena

### 1 — Colosseo, 3/4 frontale (foto con Colosseo dietro) → **cover**
```
Add two people sitting in the car: in the driver's seat, an Italian man in his 30s (the local guide) in a white linen shirt and sunglasses, one hand on the yellow steering wheel, turned toward the passenger and smiling as he points toward the Colosseum. In the passenger seat, a woman in her early 30s in a light summer dress and straw hat, laughing, looking at the Colosseum. Candid moment, not looking at the camera.
```

### 2 — Circo Massimo, vista laterale (Palatino dietro)
```
Add a couple in their late 20s seated in the car, seen in profile: a man in the driver's seat wearing a navy polo, a woman in the passenger seat with a colorful silk scarf in her hair, both looking toward the Palatine ruins, the woman holding up her phone to take a photo. Relaxed, happy holiday mood.
```

### 3 — Aventino, Priorato dei Cavalieri di Malta (vista laterale davanti al portale)
```

Behind them the ornate gate of the Knights of Malta square stays exactly as it is. Natural, mid-movement, candid.
```

### 4 — Frontale con obelischi e cipressi (Piazza Cavalieri di Malta)
```
Add two people visible through the windshield: a driver (Italian man, 30s, sunglasses, both hands on the steering wheel) and a passenger (woman, 30s, wavy hair, sunglasses on her head) leaning slightly toward each other and laughing. Faces clearly visible but natural, sunlight from behind creating a soft rim light on their hair.
```

### 5 — Retro (targa BA35 1486, strada davanti)
```
Add two people seated in the front seats, seen from behind: the passenger has her arm raised in the air, feeling the wind, hair moving slightly; the driver has one hand on the wheel. Sense of freedom and movement, as if the car is about to drive off down the road.
```

### 6 — Interno dall'alto (volante giallo, sedili marroni)
```
Add a close-up detail: a man's hand with a vintage watch resting on the yellow steering wheel, and in the passenger seat a woman's hands holding a cup of Italian gelato. Only hands and forearms visible, no faces. Warm, nostalgic, Dolce Vita feeling.
```

## Prompt di correzione (se il risultato non va)
- Persone troppo grandi/piccole: `The people are the wrong size for this car. Resize them so they fit realistically in a tiny Fiat 600: shoulders just above the door line.`
- Auto cambiata: `You changed the car. Restore the car exactly as in the original photo; only keep the people.`
- Effetto finto/plastica: `Make the people look more natural: real skin texture, slight imperfections, less posed, lighting matching the scene.`
- Mani/dita deformi: `Fix the hands: five fingers each, natural grip on the steering wheel.`
- Targa alterata: `Restore the license plate to exactly "351486 BA" as in the original.`

## Ordine consigliato per il listing (se rifatte dal vero)
1. Colosseo con coppia (cover) · 2. Priorato Cavalieri di Malta · 3. Circo Massimo · 4. Frontale obelischi · 5. Dettaglio mani/gelato · 6. Retro "partenza".
